🌿 House Manual

House Notes & Rules

These are the shared guidelines for the apartment, meant to keep things comfy, sustainable, and peaceful for everyone living here.

Everyday Living

  • 🐾 This is a pet-friendly home 🐱 Let’s talk about it together before bringing a pet into the home.
  • 👋 Please give the others a heads-up before having a guest over for a longer visit.
  • 🚭 We don’t smoke or vape indoors. An occasional smoke or vape on the balcony is okay, but not as a regular habit.
  • 🥦 You’re welcome to use the fridge, oven, dishwasher, washing machine, toiletry storage, and food storage spaces without asking.
  • 🌀 Washing machine: once a week per person, just to keep the noise down.
  • 🌙 Sundays are our quiet days: no loud activities then 😌
  • 🚪 Try to handle things gently: fridge, oven, cabinets, bedroom doors, etc.
  • 👟 We keep the place shoe-free; outside shoes stay at the door.
  • 🚽 Everyone sits when peeing, just keeps the bathroom tidier 😅
  • 📦 Please keep personal stuff in your own room. Ask first if you’d like to store things elsewhere.

Cleaning & Chores

  • 🧽 Cleaning chores are shared weekly/monthly, but flexible; swaps are fine!
  • 🌱 Watering plants is very appreciated. Just let Vlad know so nothing gets overwatered 🙈
  • 🧹 The vacuum cleaner belongs to Vlad but is free to use; please clean the nozzle and empty it after.
  • 🍽 No dishes left in the sink.
  • 🍲 Scrape leftover food before washing or using the dishwasher.
  • 🧼 Dishwasher runs only when reasonably full.
  • 🪵 No wooden items in the dishwasher.

Sustainability & Comfort

  • 🌳 We try to keep things sustainable, avoiding plastic where possible.
  • 🌱 For anything we buy for the house (cleaning products, toilet paper, batteries, etc.), let’s go for the more eco-friendly options. It makes our home a little more non-damaging to the planet 💚
  • ❄️ Heating stays at or below 19°C in winter.
  • ⚡ Electric heaters aren’t allowed.
  • ❌ Try not to leave fridge or freezer doors open for too long.

Respect & Communication

  • 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 If something gets broken, it just needs to be repaired or replaced.
  • 🧹 Everyone cleans up after themselves (and their guests).
  • 💡 Taking small initiatives is always appreciated.
  • 💬 If something feels off, bring it up so we can figure it out together.
  • 🔄 If for any reason you aren’t able to handle one of your responsibilities, let others know.
  • 🪑 Put furniture back if moved.
  • 🥤 Ask before using someone else’s things.
  • 🍳 No metal utensils on teflon.

Practical Bits

  • 🏡 The first 3 months are for settling in and seeing if we’re a good match as housemates. Let’s check in along the way to see whether living together feels calm, comfortable, and cosy for everyone 😌
  • 💶 The deposit equals your share of the monthly costs and is returned after you move out and return the keys.
  • 🧻 We share the cost of household supplies like toilet paper and cleaning products. We don’t keep an exact tally, just try to balance out what we each spend over time. It’s been working well so far!
  • 📅 Let’s aim to give each other at least 3 months’ heads-up if you decide to move out or Vlad needs the room back. That gives everyone time to plan 🌿
  • 🛋 Please check with Vlad and get his okay before making any changes to the common areas.
